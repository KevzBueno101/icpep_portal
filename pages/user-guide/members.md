## After Registration

Your account enters **PENDING** status. An admin or officer must verify and approve your membership. You'll see:

- A pending approval notice on your profile
- The ability to view announcements
- Limited access until approved

## Membership Statuses

### Editing Your Profile

Click **Edit Profile** to update:

- Name, contact number, address
- Course, year, school
- Profile picture

> Email changes require admin assistance.
